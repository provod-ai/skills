# Audience acquisition and experiment decisions

Use for the owner's actual visit/audience objective and for deciding what deserves production or expansion. Start from current site evidence and business scope. Do not make one tactic a permanent brand role or replace a visit target with article, impression, citation or revenue counts.

For each candidate record audience/task, market/engine/query source and date, comparable winners **and losers**, reachable demand/uncertainty, competition, reason to earn attention, distribution/authority dependencies, available real product supply, cost/cap, time-to-capture and falsifier. A competitor page's existence or a provider's estimated traffic is not own measured demand capture. Translation and keyword variants do not create independent demand automatically.

Compare at least two plausible mechanisms when the choice is uncertain: existing-page demand recovery; a useful standalone operation; an entity/data/integration/comparison family; a task/template library; timely reporting; or a justified authority/distribution experiment. Prioritize an existing measured opportunity if available, but never assume current traffic is zero or present without evidence. The first research output can be a win map of existing examples rather than a new article batch.

## Separate operation from acquisition

`functional_pass` admits an acquisition experiment and leaves `acquisition_unproven`. Before a cohort begins, declare its scope, observation window, minimum informative exposure, maximum maturation time, primary metric, control/comparable, cost boundary and success/futility/inconclusive rules. Derive numerical boundaries from the real baseline/market/cap; do not invent universal CTR, indexing deadlines or sample sizes.

- `invalid_measurement`: incomplete/wrong-scope data or tracking incident. Repair measurement, not the assumed content failure.
- `not_yet_exposed`: access/index eligibility/window not established. Diagnose the cause, while retaining the predeclared maximum time.
- `candidate_winner`: relevant observed net acquisition within the declared cost/time boundary, with cannibalization and channel transfers considered.
- `replicable_acquisition`: a second distinct need/cohort repeats useful yield. Hundreds of correlated URLs from one template are not hundreds of independent tests.
- `scalable`: replicated capture, enough non-overlapping demand and real supply, capacity/upkeep, cap and useful contribution toward the target.
- `futility_for_deadline`: exposure/time budget exhausted or plausible yield cannot justify the required contribution. Change the bet/edge/channel rather than manufacturing more of the same.

Keep all assets, failures and expenses in the denominator. Zero-inclusive yield is `N * (p_win * mean_win + (1-p_win) * mean_nonwin)`, capped by reachable demand/time and net of overlap/transfers. Nonwinners can still contribute traffic. A mean without distribution, hit rate, cohort age and capacity does not justify a million-visit forecast.

Primary output: ranked evidence-backed bets, explicitly unqualified candidates, predeclared experiment cards, an outcome/gap ledger and next bounded actions. Registration/use/payment and full research/build/distribution/free-compute/upkeep cost diagnose relevance/economics; do not impose positive profit as a new mandatory primary goal unless the owner chooses it. Missing metrics/cost stay unknown.

Useful mechanism sources: [Flowrite task-library case](https://flow-ai.com/blog/seo-content-strategy), [Kapwing tool case](https://www.kapwing.com/blog/getting-started-with-seo-a-guidebook/), [Google useful-content guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content). Historical operator outcomes and screenshots remain qualified, not transferred guarantees.
